Solutions · Estates & communities

Residents approve their guests. The gate admits them.

In an estate, the person who knows whether a visitor is welcome is the resident being visited. Sojaly moves the approval to the resident and leaves the gate with the easy job: admitting people who are already expected.

Day to day

How estates use Sojaly

Guest passes from the resident

A resident approves a guest in advance and the pass reaches the guest by app, email or SMS. The gate verifies the pass instead of phoning around.

Vehicles known before they arrive

Pre-registered cars and their occupants come up on the expected list, so the barrier lifts for people the estate is already expecting.

Group visits handled as one booking

A family gathering or a work crew arrives as one approved group, each person carrying their own pass from the same booking.

A live record for the estate

Management can see who is inside right now, and every entry sits in an audit trail the estate controls — including block lists for people who are not welcome back.

The products involved

Built from standard Sojaly products

Estates run Visitor Management at the gate. Because every visit is tied to a verified identity, a resident's guest also benefits: if someone ever tries to use their ID number or their car's plate at another connected gate, they find out.
