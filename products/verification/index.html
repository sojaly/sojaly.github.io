Identity & Verification

Verification services for the questions your application needs to answer

Your application does not always need someone's complete identity information. Sometimes it needs a specific answer. Sojaly provides verification services that let you ask that question and receive the result you need.

Build with SojalyTalk to us
Is this person who they claim to be?Sign in →

Let people authenticate to your application using their verified Sojaly ID.

Do they meet the age requirement?Age Verification →

Verify whether a person's verified age falls within the requirement set by your application without receiving their date of birth or identity document.

Are they accessing from an approved place?Location Verification →

Set the location conditions your service requires and receive a verification result when someone attempts to sign in. A location can be defined around a place, by an approved network, by an administrative region or by country.

Are they there right now?Presence Verification →

Ask whether an authenticated person is currently at a specified location. The response is limited to the verification result: yes, no or cannot answer.

Is this really the person performing the action?Step-up Verification →

Request another confirmation before a sensitive action. Depending on the requirement, the confirmation can include on-device face matching and an additional location condition.

Give your application the answers it needs

Use Sojaly when your application needs identity and verification without having to build every verification service itself.
