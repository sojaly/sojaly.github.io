Access & Workforce

Visitor management for the way your site actually works

Manage visitors, invitations, vehicles and access with a digital visitor management system that works across different site and hardware environments.

Request a demoSee all products
The gate

Replace the paper book without changing how the gate works

Sojaly can provide a digital visitor record while supporting workflows familiar to the people who manage access every day.

Visitors can be managed through passes, invitations, expected-arrival lists and registered vehicles.

Capabilities

Everything you need to manage visitors

Invitations

Invite visitors before they arrive and give hosts a clear view of expected arrivals.

Passes and QR codes

Use digital passes and codes to identify authorised visitors.

Group visits

Manage visits where several people are arriving together, including individual approval where required.

Vehicle registration

Pre-register vehicles and their occupants.

Live on-site records

See who is currently on site.

Block lists

Prevent specified visitors or vehicles from being admitted.

Audit trail

Maintain a record of access activity.

Data retention

Apply retention policies appropriate to the organisation.

Hardware

Works even when the gate does not have a smartphone

Sojaly Visitor Management supports different hardware and connectivity environments, including deployments where the person managing the gate has only a basic phone and SMS access.
